# 1. Quota Attainment & Accelerator Schedules

## 1.1 Annual Attainment Tiers
Commissions are calculated monthly and reconciled quarterly based on year-to-date (YTD) quota attainment against Annual Contract Value (ACV / ARR):

| YTD Quota Attainment % | Commission Rate Multiplier | Payout Frequency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0.0% – 79.9%** | 1.0x Base Commission Rate | Monthly on close + invoice |
| **80.0% – 100.0%** | 1.0x Base Commission Rate | Monthly on close + invoice |
| **100.1% – 125.0%** (Tier 1 Accelerator) | **1.5x Base Commission Rate** | Monthly on closed-won deals |
| **125.1% – 150.0%** (Tier 2 Accelerator) | **2.0x Base Commission Rate** | Monthly on closed-won deals |
| **> 150.0%** (President's Club Tier) | **2.5x Base Commission Rate** | Monthly + Executive Recognition |

## 1.2 Multi-Year Contract Incentives
- **3-Year Committed Contract (Standard)**: 100% of Year 1 ARR counts toward quota; eligible for multi-year contract bonus ($2,500 flat per contract > $100k ARR).
- **4-Year or 5-Year Contract**: 100% of Year 1 ARR + 25% of Total Contract Value (TCV) upside counted toward non-quota compensation bonus pool.
- **1-Year Contract**: Subject to a **10% commission haircut** on the base payout rate unless pre-approved by the RVP as a strategic bridge.

# 2. Modernization & Migration Incentives (IIQ-to-SaaS SPIF)

To drive the transition of on-premises IdentityIQ (IIQ) customers to Identity Security Cloud (ISC):

1. **Modernization Multiplier**: Every dollar of Net New ARR generated from an IIQ-to-SaaS conversion is credited at **1.25x quota retirement** for the territory AE.
2. **Early Bird Quarterly SPIF**:
   - Modernization deals closed in **Q1 or Q2** receive an additional **$5,000 cash bonus** per deal > $150k ARR.
   - Modernization deals closed in **Q3 or Q4** receive standard 1.25x quota retirement without the cash bonus.
3. **Migration Coordinator Incentive**: Assigned customer success modernization leads receive a milestone bonus upon successful decommissioning of on-premises infrastructure within 12 months.

# 3. Product Add-On Accelerator SPIFs

Closing specific high-priority platform modules alongside the core Identity Security Cloud suite earns immediate SPIF payouts:

- **Non-Employee Risk Management (NERM) Attach**:
  - Deals attaching NERM with ARR $\ge$ $50,000 earn an incremental **$3,000 AE SPIF** + **$1,500 SE SPIF**.
- **Data Access Security (DAS) Attach**:
  - Deals attaching DAS with ARR $\ge$ $75,000 earn an incremental **$4,000 AE SPIF** + **$2,000 SE SPIF**.
- **Cloud Infrastructure Entitlement Management (CIEM) Attach**:
  - Deals attaching CIEM earn a **$2,500 AE SPIF**.

# 4. Commission Holdback, Clawbacks & Adjustments

## 4.1 Non-Payment Clawback Rule
- If a customer defaults or fails to pay their initial invoice within **90 calendar days** of the execution date, 100% of the commission paid on that deal will be clawed back from subsequent monthly commission cycles.

## 4.2 Early Termination / De-booking
- If a customer terminates a contract for cause within the first 12 months, commission payouts are recalculated on actual realized revenue, and unearned commissions are reclaimed in the next pay period.
